# Content Quality & E-E-A-T Analysis

## E-E-A-T Framework (December 2025 QRG update)

The December 2025 QRG update extended E-E-A-T requirements to **all competitive queries**, not just YMYL. Every page on a competitive topic must demonstrate experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness.

### Experience (first-hand signals)
- Personal accounts, case studies, hands-on usage
- Original photos, screenshots, or videos
- Specific details only a practitioner would know
- "I tested this" / "We found" / "In our experience" language

### Expertise
- Author credentials relevant to the topic
- Technical accuracy and depth
- Up-to-date information (no outdated claims)
- Proper terminology used correctly

### Authoritativeness
- Author byline with credentials and bio
- Citations from other authoritative sources
- Brand mentioned by third parties
- Backlinks from relevant authoritative domains

### Trustworthiness
- Accurate, verifiable claims
- Clear editorial policy and methodology
- Contact information and about page
- Transparent about limitations and uncertainties
- Secure site (HTTPS)

## Content Metrics

### Word Count Analysis
Compare against page type minimums:
| Page Type | Minimum |
|-----------|---------|
| Homepage | 300 |
| Category/listing page | 500 |
| Product/service page | 800 |
| Blog/article | 1,000 |
| Comparison page | 1,500 |
| Pillar/hub page | 2,000 |
| About page | 400 |

> **Important:** Word count is NOT a direct ranking factor. These are topical coverage floors. Comprehensive, accurate content at any length beats padded content.

### Readability
- Flesch Reading Ease: 60-70 for general audience, 40-60 for technical topics
- Sentence length: average 15-20 words
- Paragraph length: 2-4 sentences
- Use active voice where possible

> **Note:** Readability scores are proxies, not ranking signals.

### Keyword Optimization
- Primary keyword in title, H1, first 100 words
- Natural density (1-3%)
- Semantic variations and related terms present
- No keyword stuffing

### Content Structure
- Logical heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3)
- Scannable with descriptive headings
- Tables for comparative data
- Bullet lists for multiple related items
- Numbered lists for sequential steps

### Multimedia
- Images with descriptive alt text
- Video embeds where relevant
- Infographics and charts
- Interactive tools/calculators where appropriate

### Internal Linking
- 3-5 relevant internal links per 1,000 words
- Descriptive anchor text (not "click here")
- Links to related content, parent pages, and relevant hubs
- Breadcrumbs on all non-homepage pages

### External Linking
- Link to authoritative sources to support claims
- Cite studies, official docs, primary sources
- Nofollow sponsored/affiliate links

## AI Content Assessment (December 2025 QRG)

### Acceptable AI-Assisted Content
- AI-drafted content reviewed and edited by subject matter expert
- AI-generated outlines with human-written sections
- AI-assisted research with human verification

### Low-Quality AI Content Markers (flag these)
- Generic, surface-level coverage with no unique insight
- Repetitive phrasing or unnatural sentence patterns
- Fabricated statistics or unverifiable claims
- No first-person experience or specific examples
- Identical structure across all pages (programmatic slop)

## AI Citation Readiness (GEO signals)

Optimize content for AI search engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity):

- Self-contained answer blocks (134-167 words optimal for AI citation)
- Direct answer in first 40-60 words of each section
- Specific facts and statistics with sources
- Question-based H2/H3 headings
- FAQ sections with clear Q&A format
- Author entity with credentials (Person schema)

## Content Freshness

- Publication date visible on all articles
- Last-updated date on evergreen content
- Flag pages with no update in 12+ months on competitive topics
- Time-sensitive content (prices, stats) updated when source changes
